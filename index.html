YMCA eHub: co-designing an inclusive gaming space

Participant recruitment · Research team member

A human-centred project for Laurier Brantford YMCA: an inclusive gaming hub (eHub) for 18–23-year-olds, designed to help young people feel welcome, build social connection and ease loneliness.

We ran co-creation sessions in which 7 participants arranged a paper sandbox room using furniture models and explained their choices. Their feedbacks helped shape our low-fidelity model. We know that 7 sessions is a small sample, so we treat these patterns as direction, not proof.

In the mid-fidelity phase, we will upgrade the cardboard model with 3D printing and test it with users.

I am responsible for visual communication and for recruiting participants for interviews. By using clear visuals and infographics I present each phase’s results and feedback directly to our client.

International graduate student experience

Research team member · Interviewer · Notetaker · Participant analysis

A user research project exploring international graduate students' experiences with campus activities and their participation in university life. Our goal is to better understand students' needs, challenges, and motivations when engaging with campus activities.

As part of the research team, I contributed to developing the research plan, defining participant groups, identifying recruitment channels, and preparing interview and survey questions. I also participate in user interviews as an interviewer or note-taker, coordinate team activities, organize research materials, and communicate research progress with our client.

The project is currently in the interview stage, where we are gathering first-hand insights to better understand the experiences of international graduate students.

Team of 53-min film

imagine: directing a team of five

Director · Scriptwriter · Producer · Animator

A three-minute mixed-media animated short created by a five-person team for OCAD University's Studio House course.

As Director and Producer, I led the project from the initial idea to the final film, working on the pitch deck, storyboard, animatic, animation, and production schedule. With less than four months to complete the project, I set a realistic scope and chose mixed media to let each team member use their strengths in 3D animation, stop-motion, and frame-by-frame animation.

I assigned shots based on each person's skills and interests, coordinated the team's creative direction, tracked progress, and helped resolve disagreements. Together, we successfully completed the film within the stipulated time.

About

Hi, I'm Renee!

I'm a UX designer with a background in experimental animation. My journey into UX began with an interest in how people experience and interpret visual information. Through animation, I learned to think about the audience behind every frame. Now, I bring that same curiosity into understanding users, identifying their needs, and making everyday experiences easier to navigate.

I enjoy exploring problems from different perspectives and working with people from different backgrounds and disciplines. I believe good design starts with understanding the people it serves, rather than making assumptions about what they need.

With a background that bridges animation and UX, I bring together visual storytelling, research, and creative problem-solving to create thoughtful and accessible experiences.
